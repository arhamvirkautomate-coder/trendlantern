How the backtest works

  • It replays the short-term scoring model candle by candle over roughly the last four months of 4-hour prices. Each step only uses data available at that time.
  • A simulated trade starts when the score reaches "Strong uptrend" with confirmed momentum. It ends at the volatility stop (2×ATR), half at +1.5R and the rest at +3R, or after 10 days.
  • Fees of 0.1% each way are included. News is not included, because there is no reliable news history.
  • "Model account" assumes one-third of a model account per trade. "Buy & hold" is the coin's own change over the same period.
  • Backtests describe the past. They do not predict the future and are not a recommendation to trade.

Common risk-management principles

  • Many traders limit the loss on any single trade to 1–2% of their account.
  • Leverage magnifies losses as well as gains. Spot positions cannot be liquidated.
  • Meme and low-liquidity coins can move 20–50% in a day.
  • Exchange accounts can be hacked or frozen. Many people keep only active trading funds on exchanges.
  • Crypto gains are taxable in many countries. Keep records of every trade.

How it works

Every 4 hours, automatically

  1. Collects prices and 4-hour and daily candles for the ~40 largest liquid coins on Binance, plus the largest meme coins.
  2. Reads crypto, macro and regulation headlines and scores each one for sentiment and likely impact.
  3. Adds market-wide data: Fear & Greed, BTC dominance, stablecoin supply, DeFi TVL and market breadth.
  4. Scores every coin twice. The short-term score uses the 4-hour trend, RSI, MACD, volume, Bollinger bands, news and the Bitcoin backdrop. The long-term score uses the 200-day trend, golden/death cross, strength against BTC, drawdown, size, volatility and sentiment.
  5. Calculates technical reference levels: pullback zone, a volatility stop at 2×ATR, +1.5R and +3R levels, and long-term trend bands.
  6. Replays the short-term model on past prices (Backtests tab) so you can see how it behaved.
  7. An AI model reads the data and headlines and writes the market brief. It is instructed not to give recommendations.
  8. Every Strong uptrend and Downtrend label is logged and graded 1, 3 and 7 days later on the Report card.

    Limits you should know

    • Scores come from fixed rules applied to public data. They can't foresee hacks, regulation, delistings or sudden news.
    • News sentiment is scored by keywords and can misread a headline.
    • Data can be late, missing or wrong. Always check prices on your exchange before acting.
